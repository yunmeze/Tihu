## Repeat versus mirrored repeat
Both are legitimate world-UV sampling modes, with immutable raw RGB. Ordinary repeat wraps u modulo 1; mirror-repeat alternates u and 1-u every cycle (same for v). The latter can remove the numerical wrap discontinuity because the neighboring boundary samples match, but may create obvious reflected flowers, gravel pairs or symmetric dark lobes. Therefore both modes require native 2×2 and same-world-field inspection. A mirror-repeat success would be labeled sampling-assisted continuity, not naturally seamless generation. Do not shorten the period just to average away coarse art, or stretch a low-resolution image across the whole map.
