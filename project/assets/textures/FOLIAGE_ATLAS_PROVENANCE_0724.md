Created on 2026-10-13 with the built-in image generation tool. No API keys, external texture libraries, or third-party image assets were used. Project look development was visually consulted for style; it was not used as runtime evidence. The selected PNG contains the original generated pixels, copied without repainting, resampling, alpha cleanup, compositing, or color conversion.

The atlas contains sixteen separate connected plant silhouettes in four rows and four columns. It combines three grass clumps, three leafy sprays, two fern-like sprays, three flower clusters, two ivy/climbing-groundcover clumps, and three mixed plants. Painted leaf planes use warm yellow-green highlights, medium leaf green and cool teal-green bases. Blossoms are cream and gold. Fern subjects came back with broad close-set leaflets, so use them as stylized fern-like plants rather than expecting finely divided botanical ferns.

## Integration contract

The Blender owner authors one gently bowed, subdivided card with full 0–1 UVs and bottom-center origin. Runtime may reuse this authored card in staggered or crossed groups. No visible source geometry is generated by this texture deliverable.

Use the per-tile `atlas_region_xywh` from the JSON rather than full equal-cell UVs. Coordinates are image top-down: card top samples local UV y=0, card base samples local UV y=1. Each suggested region adds 3 image pixels at the top and sides and ends at the visible silhouette bottom. Preserve sprite pixel aspect when scaling the card. Typical whole-clump height is 0.13–0.46 meters; detailed ranges are in the JSON. A few ivy and mixed-leaf silhouettes have lower hanging side tips; the estimated central-root support height can be used when placing at a ledge. Root estimates are measured silhouette support positions, not guaranteed anatomical anchors.

Read the texture as sRGB (`source_color`), keep alpha, enable mipmaps, use linear mipmapped sampling and disable texture repeat. Use neutral white or a modest tint multiplier rather than multiplying by a second dark leaf-green base color. The art already contains green pigment and broad leaf-plane variation; avoid strongly darkening it again.

Use alpha-scissor around 0.45. The generation contains a real alpha channel, but also very-low-alpha green/yellow fringe and near-zero-alpha specks. Unrestricted alpha blending or alpha hash can expose that fringe. At alpha >= 115/255, all sixteen visible plant silhouettes remain clear of their equal-cell borders, and zero strongly saturated green fringe pixels survive the numerical color check. The original alpha pixels are preserved, not claimed to be hand-cleaned. Test silhouette quality and mipmap behavior in the actual renderer.
