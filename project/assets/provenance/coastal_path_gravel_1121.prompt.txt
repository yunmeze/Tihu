Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original tileable-intent hand-painted coastal path gravel diffuse albedo for a stylized game. ONE flat square 1024 x 1024 RGB texture, opaque.
Primary request: pale cream and sandy ochre packed ground with sparse tiny flat warm pebbles and angular chips, gathered into unequal loose little groups with substantial clear calmer spaces between groups. The texture will be applied only to narrow irregular margins of a small seaside path. It must read as quiet secondary detail under a charming stylized character, not as a noisy decorative carpet.
Style/medium: restrained matte gouache-like hand-painted game texture; simple softly angular chip silhouettes with flat pigment, mostly smooth ground with only a few extremely subtle broad painted color variations. Warm soft storybook style. At least 85 percent of the area remains calm nearly plain cream-sand ground. Pebbles occupy no more than about 8 percent of the tile. Form irregular groupings of 3 to 8 tiny chips separated by broad empty zones; vary group size, spacing and density. Most individual chips are about 3 to 9 pixels wide on a 1024 pixel tile, with a very few 12 pixel pieces. Few-dozen groupings across the full tile, not a uniform grid or evenly spaced polka dots.
Color palette: pale cream-sand background centered near #e8d2aa with low-contrast restrained cream, muted sandy ochre, warm beige and pale peach pebble pigments. Light warm chip edges are allowed as flat pigment variation; avoid dark brown or black material. Keep the albedo bright, light, warm and subtly varied.
Composition/framing: flat front-on orthographic ground surface filling the entire square, no horizon or perspective. Seamless tiling intent in both axes, matching colors and feature continuity on opposite boundaries. Distribute the same materials to all image edges without a border or vignette.
Lighting: pure base-color/albedo with completely even illumination. NO cast shadows, NO directional highlights, NO ambient-occlusion halos, NO raised bevel shading. Chips are flat pigment shapes embedded in the ground, not shaded three-dimensional rocks.
Avoid: photographic sand grains, dense grainy noise, tiny black speckling, gritty pepper, sharp dark outlines, large rocks, cobblestones, cracked slabs, dirt clods, plants, grass, moss, footprints, ruts, tracks, a road scene, objects, typography, watermark.
